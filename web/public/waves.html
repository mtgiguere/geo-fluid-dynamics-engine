Geo-Fluid Dynamics Engine · wave player (draft)

Watch an idea travel

Pick an idea and press play. Each county darkens as the idea fills it in. The orange flash is the moment a county crosses the mark (a set share of its own final total): the edge of the wave.

Each county is coloured by the share of its own final total reached so far, so a big county and a small one are read by the shape of their arrival, not their size. The line under the map is the share of counties past the mark: a wave draws an S-curve over months, a broadcast draws a cliff.

"Learning ratio": in the hazard model, how much more a county is pulled by neighbours who went in the last few weeks than by neighbours who go in the next few (which it could not have learned from); 95% ranges from resampling states. 1 means no neighbour-to-neighbour spread. Sources: Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory, Tracking the Sun (solar); FEC, ActBlue's raw filings (donors, counted by ZIP and day; no names kept); Census ZIP-county file. Alaska, Hawaii and Puerto Rico not shown.
